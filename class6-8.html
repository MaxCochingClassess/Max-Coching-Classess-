ENGLISH

📘 English Grammar

1. Parts of Speech

Noun — नाम बताने वाला शब्द

Pronoun — Noun के स्थान पर आने वाला शब्द

Verb — काम/action बताता है

Adjective — Noun की विशेषता

Adverb — Verb की विशेषता

Preposition — संबंध बताता है

Conjunction — शब्द/वाक्य जोड़ता है

Interjection — अचानक भावना

Article — a, an, the

Noun

Common / Proper / Collective / Abstract

Singular: boy

Plural: boys

Gender: Masculine, Feminine, Common, Neuter

Pronoun

I, we, you, he, she, it, they

Example:

Ram is a boy.
He is a boy.

Verb

Action या state बताने वाला शब्द।

go, eat, play, read, write

Adjective

Noun की quality बताता है।

Ram is a good boy.

Adverb

He runs fast.

Preposition

The book is on the table.

Conjunction

Ram and Shyam are friends.

Interjection

Wow! What a beautiful flower!

Articles

A boy
An apple
The sun

⏰ Tenses

Present

I play.

I am playing.

I have played.

Past

I played.

I was playing.

I had played.

Future

I will play.

I will be playing.

I will have played.

🔑 Modal Verbs

Can — ability

Could — past ability/request

May — permission/possibility

Might — possibility

Must — necessity

Should — advice

Would — polite request

🔄 Active & Passive Voice

Active: Ram eats mango.
Passive: Mango is eaten by Ram.

💬 Direct & Indirect Speech

Direct: Ram said, "I am happy."
Indirect: Ram said that he was happy.
