🔢 Number System

संख्याओं को उनके गुणों के आधार पर अलग-अलग प्रकार में समझा जाता है।

Natural Numbers: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5...

Whole Numbers: 0, 1, 2, 3, 4...

Prime Numbers: 2, 3, 5, 7, 11...

Composite Numbers: 4, 6, 8, 9, 10...

➕ Integers

... -3, -2, -1, 0, 1, 2, 3 ...

5 + (-2) = 3
3 - 5 = -2

🍕 Fractions — भिन्न

भिन्न में ऊपर की संख्या अंश और नीचे की संख्या हर कहलाती है।

2/3 + 4/5

LCM of 3 and 5 = 15
2/3 = 10/15
4/5 = 12/15

उत्तर = 22/15
4/2 − 4/3
= 2 − 4/3
= 2/3
2/5 × 9/4
= 18/20
= 9/10
4/5 ÷ 4/7
= 4/5 × 7/4
= 7/5

🔢 Decimals — दशमलव

0.5 = 5/10
0.25 = 25/100
1.50 + 2.25 = 3.75

⚖️ Ratio & Proportion

2 : 3

अगर 2 लड़के और 3 लड़कियाँ हैं, तो लड़के : लड़कियाँ = 2 : 3

2 : 3 = 4 : 6

💯 Percentage

Percentage = (Value ÷ Total) × 100

20% of 250
= 20/100 × 250
= 50

💰 Profit & Loss — लाभ और हानि

CP = Cost Price
SP = Selling Price

Profit = SP − CP
Loss = CP − SP

अगर CP = ₹100 और SP = ₹120
Profit = 120 − 100 = ₹20

🏷️ Discount — छूट

Marked Price = ₹500
Discount = 10%

Discount = 10/100 × 500
= ₹50

Selling Price = ₹500 − ₹50
= ₹450

🔤 Algebra — बीजगणित

x + 5 = 12

x = 12 − 5
x = 7

🟰 Simple Equations

2x = 10
x = 10 ÷ 2
x = 5

📏 Lines & Angles

Straight Angle = 180°
Right Angle = 90°
Acute Angle < 90°
Obtuse Angle > 90° और < 180°

🔺 Triangle — त्रिभुज

Triangle के तीनों angles का योग: 180°

Types:
Equilateral

⬛ Quadrilateral — चतुर्भुज

Square

📐 Mensuration

Rectangle Area = Length × Breadth

Square Area = Side × Side

Rectangle Perimeter = 2(L + B)

Square Perimeter = 4 × Side

📊 Data Handling

Data को table, pictograph और bar graph के माध्यम से समझा जा सकता है।

जब किसी आकृति को एक रेखा से दो समान भागों में बाँटा जा सके, तो उसे symmetry कहते हैं।

🔤 Parts of Speech

Noun: नाम बताने वाला शब्द

Pronoun: Noun के स्थान पर

Verb: काम या अवस्था

Adjective: Noun की विशेषता

Adverb: Verb की विशेषता

Preposition: संबंध बताता है

Conjunction: शब्द/वाक्य जोड़ता है

Interjection: अचानक भावना

Article: A, An, The

⏰ Tenses

Type Example
Present I play.
Past I played.
Future I will play.

🔑 Modal Verbs

Can — ability

Could — past ability / polite request

May — permission

Might — possibility

Must — necessity

Should — advice

Would — polite request

🔄 Active & Passive Voice

Active: Ram eats mango.

Passive: Mango is eaten by Ram.

💬 Direct & Indirect Speech

Direct: Ram said, "I am happy."

Indirect: Ram said that he was happy.

🔤 Synonyms & Antonyms

Word Synonym Antonym
Big Large Small

Fast Quick Slow
Easy Simple Difficult

📝 Types of Sentence

Interrogative — Are you ready?

Imperative — Open the door.

Exclamatory — What a beautiful day!

Ram is playing cricket.
